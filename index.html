FAQ

Before you enrol.

Clear expectations make better students and better service work.

Who is this Masterclass for?

Beginners who want a structured start, working technicians who want to improve software workflow, and learners moving toward advanced memory/programming concepts.

Who is Arif Rennell?

Arif Rennell is a mobile software technician and trainer behind Rennell Software Masterclass, with 7+ years of practical experience and a teaching focus on structured, professional mobile software workflows.

Is this hardware repair training?

The core focus is mobile software and programming concepts. Some hardware-adjacent topics are included where they are necessary to understand software diagnosis, storage and servicing workflow.

Will I learn every phone model?

No course can responsibly promise every current or future model. The goal is to teach transferable principles, chipset workflows and troubleshooting methods that help you learn supported models faster.

Is the training for lawful service work?

Yes. Students should work only on devices, accounts and data they own or are explicitly authorized to service. Training is for educational, professional and lawful servicing purposes.

How do I get fees and batch details?

Course structure, current pricing, schedule and enrollment terms can change. Contact Arif Rennell directly for the current batch information.
